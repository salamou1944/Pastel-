# PASTEL Host — Customer Quickstart

## Goal
Turn one verified business into a sellable QR + smart host page without rebuilding the runtime.

## Customer package
- One public URL
- One printable QR destination
- Mobile-first business page
- Verified business knowledge
- Arabic/French chat
- Voice fallback
- 3D host
- WhatsApp / phone / email / directions

## Required customer inputs
1. Business name
2. Logo or approved visual identity
3. Verified address
4. Verified phone / WhatsApp
5. Verified email (optional)
6. Verified services
7. Verified products/menu
8. Preferred Arabic/French presentation
9. Approved avatar/asset rights

## Production rule
Do not publish or replace a customer's QR destination until the public URL is live and tested on a real phone.

## Fast delivery sequence
1. Copy `host-standard/` as the customer delivery surface.
2. Edit only `config.js` and approved assets.
3. Run source checks.
4. Deploy through the Pages workflow.
5. Verify the public URL from a real phone.
6. Generate/print the QR for that URL.
7. Deliver the QR + URL to the customer.

## Commercial boundary
The first paid offer does not require a paid LLM. The baseline can use verified deterministic answers and browser voice. LLM integration is an upgrade, not a prerequisite.

## Do not claim
- live reservations
- live availability
- live prices
- delivery availability
- AI-generated facts

unless independently verified and implemented.
